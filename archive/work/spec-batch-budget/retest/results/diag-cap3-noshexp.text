## 1. Overview

The NimbusTech Job Queue (NTJQ) is a distributed, durable work-processing system. Producers enqueue jobs; workers dequeue, execute, and acknowledge them. The system guarantees at-least-once delivery, bounded latency under load, and graceful degradation when downstream services fail.
